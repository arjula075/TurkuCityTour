## Description (English)

Explore Turku on foot with TurkuCityTour — a location-based tour game for groups and events.

Follow hints on the map, walk to real landmarks, and answer questions when you arrive. Your organiser assigns your tour; progress syncs automatically.

Features:
- Live map with your GPS position
- Step-by-step hints and arrival detection
- Quiz questions at each stop
- Photo gallery when you finish

Location is used only during active play to verify you reached each stop.
